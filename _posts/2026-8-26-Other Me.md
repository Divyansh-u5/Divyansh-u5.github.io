yeh This is other me who writes these stuff also .  AS APJ Abdul Kalam said *" Poetry comes from the highest happiness or the deepest sorrow "*   Never Know Why but every time by Heart .

Ok This is the most recent I Wrote .**Mai khush Huin** in this there is a situation where a man has fallen crush or love with someone and he is happy that he can see her smiling although He is also Afraid of what if She get to Know and She gets upset and he could not see her again ....

***Main Khush Huin***

<div style="text-align: left;">
Main khush huin,<br>
ki roz uski Muskarahat ka deedar kar leta huin.<br>
Man hi man kuchh keh deta huin.<br>

Dar nhi hai , uske door jane ka. <br>
Kyunki , Roz uska deedar kar leta hui,.<br>
Muskarahat uski dil ke ek bakse me rakh leta huin .<br>
Man hi Man kuchh Keh deta huin.<br>

Dar nhi hai, door jane Ka,<br>
Pr Dar hai, uske Pass ane Ka.<br>
Jo man me kaha,<br>
Bo juban pein ane ka.<br>
Muskarahat uski Kho jane Ka.<br>
Aur dil Ke mere Ruk Jane ka ....<br>

                                                      -Divyanshu-
</div>

Hm so Do you Like it , yeh the Rhyming is not that good I know but the next is really a fine piece of words .

How things could just change, How the monotonic life could have most beautiful color palette. This is how everything happens - When She comes **TERE AJANE SE**.


 ***TERE AJANE SE***

 <div style="text-align: center;">
Kisi ki Neende udd jati hai,<br>
To kisi ki Ratein ban Jati Hai.<br>

Oo meri Chand.<br> 
Tere Ajane se,<br>
Amavas ki Andheri rat bhi,<br>
Poonam Ki Chandni ho Jaati Hai.<br>


Oo meri heeriye.<br>
Tere Muskurane se,<br>
Is ranhje Ki Her Haar  bhi, <br>
Jeet ban jati hai<br>

Oo meri Chand.<br> 
Tere Ajane se,<br>
Is ruke Dil ki,<br>
Dhadkan vapas jee Jaati Hai.<br>

                                         -Divyanshu-
</div>
