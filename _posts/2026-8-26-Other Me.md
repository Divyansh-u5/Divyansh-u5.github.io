***Yeh Ishq Kya Kya Karata Hai***

<div style="text-align: left;">
Ek aspatal me ...<br>
Kitna zeher , kaunsa zeher , kyu pia doctor ne puchha<br>
Kisi ne bola , yeh zeher , pura piya , pagal ho gyi hai<br>

Yeh sab mai dekh raha tha<br>
man me apne soch raha tha<br>

Are mar jane do usko<br>
chain se jeene nhi dia<br>
chain se mar to jane do usko<br>
sirf zeher nhi pia hai usne<br>
pia hai zeher isq ka usne<br>
jan bacha bhi loge uski<br>
jee payegi , kya bo<br>

Kahani yehi khatam ho jati<br>
Chain se bo mar jati<br>

Lekin fir isq jeet jata na<br>
Gore samaj ka kala chehra bahar ata na<br>
khair sayad bhach gyi hai jan uski<br>
lekin kya jee payegi bo<br>

is sab ke bad ek sawal ata hai mere man ab bhi<br>
yeh ishq kya kya karata hai<br>
Raja ko ghutno pe lata hai<br>
samundar ko bhi sant jheel banata hai<br>
Zinda ko murda<br>
Aur murda ko jinda rahgeer banata<br>
yeh isq kya kya karata hai<br>

                                                      -Divyanshu-
</div>


This was My perspective and now it is parents perspective .

***Main Khush Huin***

<div style="text-align: left;">
Main khush huin,<br>
ki roz uski Muskarahat ka deedar kar leta huin.<br>
Man hi man kuchh keh deta huin.<br>

Dar nhi hai , uske door jane ka. <br>
Kyunki , Roz uska deedar kar leta hui,.<br>
Muskarahat uski dil ke ek bakse me rakh leta huin .<br>
Man hi Man kuchh Keh deta huin.<br>

Dar nhi hai, door jane Ka,<br>
Pr Dar hai, uske Pass ane Ka.<br>
Jo man me kaha,<br>
Bo juban pein ane ka.<br>
Muskarahat uski Kho jane Ka.<br>
Aur dil Ke mere Ruk Jane ka ....<br>

                                                      -Divyanshu-
</div>

How things could just change, How the monotonic life could have most beautiful color palette. This is how everything happens - When She comes **TERE AJANE SE**.


 ***TERE AJANE SE***

 <div style="text-align: center;">
Kisi ki Neende udd jati hai,<br>
To kisi ki Ratein ban Jati Hai.<br>

Oo meri Chand.<br> 
Tere Ajane se,<br>
Amavas ki Andheri rat bhi,<br>
Poonam Ki Chandni ho Jaati Hai.<br>


Oo meri heeriye.<br>
Tere Muskurane se,<br>
Is ranhje Ki Her Haar  bhi, <br>
Jeet ban jati hai<br>

Oo meri Chand.<br> 
Tere Ajane se,<br>
Is ruke Dil ki,<br>
Dhadkan vapas jee Jaati Hai.<br>

                                         -Divyanshu-
</div>

When some time you love someone and they are gone . But you still believe that one day they will return . In reality no one returns , 
You think, you are waiting for them but reality knows you are only waiting for your death . A tragic Happy death . 

***INTEZAR ME USKE***

<div style="text-align: center;">
Rat andheri me beeti ja rahi,<br>
is sham me, uski kiran dhal ti ja rahi,<br>
intezar me uske, ghadi ki ret baheti ja rahi.<br>
<br>
Kisi ne kaha, subah ayegi bo,<br>
lekin intezar me, us subah ki,<br>
meri rooh ki awaj dabi si ja rahin,<br>
sanse is deh ki, thami si ja rahin.<br>
<br>
Rat beeti to, subah ayi to,<br>
lekin bo na ayi, aur jab ayi bo,<br>
tab rooh ki awaj dab gyi thi,<br>
deh ki sanse thham gyi thi,<br>
intezar me uske, ghadi ki ret ab ruk gyi thi.<br>


                                         -Divyanshu-
</div>
